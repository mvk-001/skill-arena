# Plan knowledge expertise before Harbor evolution

## Status

Accepted on 2026-07-28.

## Context

The repository's evolvers optimize complete skills from native Harbor evidence,
but none owns a domain-neutral contract for translating knowledge-expert failure
patterns into a balanced mutation portfolio. A caller can otherwise overfit one
aggregate retrieval score, leak task facts into operator text, ignore evidence
fidelity or robustness, or mistake an exposed cohort for promotion evidence.

This missing planning boundary is distinct from evaluation. Harbor rewards,
candidate realization, Pareto selection, operator credit, and holdout promotion
already have separate owning skills and must not be duplicated.

## Decision

Add the atomic `harbor-maximize-knowledge-expertise` bundle.

The bundle:

- binds a complete target skill and sanitized development evidence by SHA-256;
- requires at least three declared expertise dimensions, including evidence
  fidelity and robustness;
- accepts only opaque case IDs and a fixed failure-mode vocabulary;
- maps those gaps to fixed, benchmark-agnostic mutation instructions;
- preserves a conservative branch and multiple operator families;
- delegates realization and all native Harbor decisions to existing bundles;
  and
- marks plans without an untouched holdout as retrospective and
  promotion-ineligible.

Its standard-library planner computes no reward, reads no metric value, creates
no candidate, calls no model or Harbor runtime, and refuses output overwrite.
The plan is deterministic, source-bound, and self-digested.

## Consequences

- Knowledge-expert campaigns gain a reusable multi-dimensional planning stage
  without adding a second evaluation layer.
- Operator text cannot contain questions, answers, qrels, case IDs, or holdout
  facts because it comes from a fixed generic library.
- A plan still requires separately realized candidates and fresh native Harbor
  jobs before any improvement claim.
- An `unavailable` holdout permits retrospective development evidence only. A
  `frozen-unopened` declaration remains promotion-unverified until the owning
  Harbor gate runs.
- The maintained atomic bundle count increases from ten to eleven.
