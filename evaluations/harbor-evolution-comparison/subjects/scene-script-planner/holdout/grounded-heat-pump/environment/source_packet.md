## SRC-HP-01

- Title: Heat Pump Systems
- Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy
- URL: https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/heat-pump-systems
- Evidence: Heat pumps transfer heat between a building and the outdoors and can provide both heating and cooling.

## SRC-HP-02

- Title: Heat pumps
- Publisher: International Energy Agency
- URL: https://www.iea.org/energy-system/buildings/heat-pumps
- Evidence: Heat pumps use a refrigeration cycle and electricity to move heat; performance depends on system and operating conditions.
